# Financial Performance Analysis

Explain material movements, supported drivers, unexplained effects and their financing implications.

Apply [operating-rules.md](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and [workflow-contract.md](../../shared/workflow-contract.md). Use the approved spread and relevant statements, notes, interim accounts and operating evidence. Use suitable institutional methods for sector-specific or regulated-capital cases.

Tools: [register.py](../../tools/register.py), [fin_table.py](../../tools/fin_table.py).

## Analyse

Consult relevant sections of [financial-analysis-methods.md](../../shared/financial-analysis-methods.md) for comparisons, bridges, adjustments and cash conversion.

Match entity, perimeter, periods, currency/scale and definitions; distinguish actual, forecast and adjusted figures. Financial-table mappings need located identity or a reviewed external basis agreeing with displayed units; retain formula-cache checks.

Use deterministic calculations; retain formulas, located inputs and rounding in reusable code or an editable workbook. Label unverifiable calculations provisional and limit reliance on them.

Select the movements that matter to the case:

- **Revenue and margins:** operating drivers, perimeter/FX changes, accounting effects and earnings quality. Sales growth alone does not establish profitability or collection.
- **Cash conversion:** profit-to-cash reconciliation and working-capital quality, including seasonality and temporary releases.
- **Investment and funding:** capex, distributions, acquisitions, financing flows and cash restrictions. Separate maintenance/growth capex only where supported.

Test management explanations against notes, operating data and later evidence, preserving attribution unless corroborated. A reconciled bridge still needs a plausible mechanism.

## Deliver

Return located conclusions about earning power, cash conversion and implications for the current question, with checked calculations, contrary/unexplained evidence and the basis for recurring/temporary assessments or recovery assumptions. The evidence owner integrates them into the register; retain detailed calculations internally. Use repayment-and-structure for material liquidity, maturity or forward repayment questions. Historical EBITDA alone does not establish debt-service capacity.
