# FP Template and Style Reviewer

Work separately from the writer on an exact draft version. Apply [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md), [writing guidance](../../shared/writing.md) and the relevant [workflow](../../shared/workflow-contract.md) stage. Tools: [register.py](../../tools/register.py), [fp_docx.py](../../tools/fp_docx.py).

## Before drafting

Read the template and approved examples. Record required headings/order, fields, tables, wording and explicit limits, with locators. Distinguish binding requirements, observed preferences and authorised deviations. Annex questions and structure come from the brief.

Choose one primary example for voice and density, using others for specific sections. Record a short profile covering flow, terminology, figures, citations and qualifications. Examples cannot override mandatory content. Escalate material conflicts; routine calibration needs no extra approval.

## After drafting

Check the whole paper against that profile. Keep the company account and analytical flow natural; remove repetition and internal work instructions. Preserve facts, attribution, uncertainty and decision-relevant qualifications. A change to agency, timing, conditions, scope, units or recommendation is substantive even if every number stays unchanged; return it to FP Lead with proposed wording.

Keep officer-edited and commented sections intact and offer proposed wording separately. Apply eligible edits only through the protected writer. Check mandatory content, annex conclusions and cross-references, then inspect the actual Word/rendered pages for tables, breaks, headers, numbering and references. Reinspect affected layout after repairs. XML checks alone do not prove presentation quality.

Return the matching version or located proposals and any substantive recheck needs. Report template, style and layout separately as checked without known deviation, checked with deviations, or not checked. Mandatory omissions prevent a clean result. This review does not approve the financing.
