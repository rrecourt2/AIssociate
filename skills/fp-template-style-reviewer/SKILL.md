# Template and Style Reviewer

Apply [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md), [writing guidance](../../shared/writing.md) and the [evidence workflow](../../shared/workflow-contract.md). Tool: [register.py](../../tools/register.py). Document Lead owns the document and its editing.

## Before drafting

Document Lead applies this step in the main task. Read the applicable template and examples for this document type. Record the required structure, fields, tables, wording and limits with locators, and distinguish requirements, preferences and authorised deviations. Term Sheets follow the supplied template. Do not impose FP structure or depth on a scan or CIP.

Choose one primary example for voice and density and use others only for specific sections. Record a short profile of its flow, terminology, figures, citations and qualifications in Analysis key `preflight`. Examples cannot override mandatory content. Escalate material conflicts; routine calibration needs no extra approval. Without a template or example, say so instead of claiming house-style calibration.

## After drafting

Run as a separate agent on the exact saved version. Check the whole document against its purpose, audience, length and profile. Remove repetition and internal work instructions. Preserve facts, attribution and material uncertainty. Changes to agency, timing, conditions, scope, units or the recommendation are substantive: return them to Document Lead with proposed wording. Do not restyle negotiated clauses.

Keep the Investment Officer's edits, tracked changes and comments intact; return corrections as tracked changes or located proposals. Check tracked edits and comment anchors in the saved Word file; a clean-text comparison is not enough. Inspect mandatory content, cross-references and the rendered layout, and re-inspect repairs. External copies keep review markup intended for their recipients but exclude internal-only comments, revisions and revealing metadata or links.

Report template, style and layout separately as checked without known deviation, checked with deviations, or not checked. Mandatory omissions prevent a clean result. This review does not approve the financing.
