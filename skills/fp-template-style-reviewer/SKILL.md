# Template and Style Reviewer

Work separately from the writer on an exact draft version. Apply [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md), [writing guidance](../../shared/writing.md) and the [evidence workflow](../../shared/workflow-contract.md). Document Lead owns the build/editing route. Tools: [register.py](../../tools/register.py), [fp_docx.py](../../tools/fp_docx.py).

## Before drafting

Read the applicable template and examples for this document type. Record required structure, fields, tables, wording and limits with locators; distinguish requirements, preferences and authorised deviations. Term Sheets follow the supplied template. Do not impose FP structure or depth on a scan or CIP.

Choose one primary example for voice and density, using others for specific sections. Record a short profile covering flow, terminology, figures, citations and qualifications. Examples cannot override mandatory content. Escalate material conflicts; routine calibration needs no extra approval.

## After drafting

Check the whole document against its purpose, audience, length and profile. Remove repetition and internal work instructions. Preserve facts, attribution and material uncertainty. Changes to agency, timing, conditions, scope, units or recommendation are substantive; return them to Document Lead with proposed wording. Do not stylistically rewrite negotiated clauses.

Keep Investment Officer edits, revisions and comments intact. Return corrections through the chosen protected/tracked route or as proposals. Verify tracked edits and anchored comments in the saved Word; a clean-text diff is insufficient. Inspect mandatory content, cross-references and rendered layout; reinspect repairs. External copies retain intended review markup, but exclude internal-only comments, revisions and revealing metadata/links.

Return the matching version or located proposals and any substantive recheck needs. Report template, style and layout separately as checked without known deviation, checked with deviations, or not checked. Mandatory omissions prevent a clean result. This review does not approve the financing.
