# Annual Report Review

Establish what the report discloses and what was actually read. Apply [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and the [evidence workflow](../../shared/workflow-contract.md). Tool: [register.py](../../tools/register.py).

## Establish scope and coverage

Identify the report and version, reporting entity, consolidated or standalone boundary, fiscal period, currency and scale, audit status and publication date. Distinguish the group from the borrower and guarantors. Record PDF page numbers and printed page labels when they differ.

Map every agreed part to its pages: directors' and auditor's reports, statements, policies, notes and other included sections. A whole-report request covers the whole report; a narrower scope must be explicit. Reuse an earlier review only for the same report version and within its reviewed scope.

Read every required part, following table continuations, headers, footnotes and cross-references. Record each part as read, pending or unreadable. Extraction alone is not substantive review.

When extraction loses signs, columns, scale or scanned text, inspect the page images or available OCR and compare them with the source. If that fails, keep the gap. Never mark an unreadable passage as not disclosed or infer a zero from an empty cell.

## Review the disclosures

Check these disclosures while completing the agreed reading:

- audit opinion, key audit matters, going concern and subsequent events;
- accounting policies and changes, estimates, restatements and consolidation changes;
- revenue, segments, costs, exceptional items, provisions and impairment;
- receivables, inventories, payables, cash restrictions and cash-flow presentation;
- borrowings, leases, maturities, interest and FX exposure, covenants, security, breaches and waivers;
- related parties, ownership, guarantees, commitments, contingencies, litigation and tax.

Record each as found with a locator, not applicable with a basis, not disclosed after the relevant reading, or unresolved. Follow contradictions and cross-references; keep positive context as well as risk.

Compare material statement and note amounts with the spread only after matching entity, period, definitions and units. Keep reported and adjusted values, and restated and originally published comparatives, apart. Use deterministic arithmetic for tie-outs; record explained differences and unresolved residuals without inserting plugs.

## Deliver

Return the coverage, located material findings with the decisive passage or table and its qualifications, reconciliation issues and evidence gaps. Digests help navigation; they do not replace source evidence. An audited report does not verify every management explanation or forecast.
