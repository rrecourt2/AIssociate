# Annual Report Review

Establish what the report discloses and what was actually read. Apply [operating-rules.md](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and [workflow-contract.md](../../shared/workflow-contract.md).

Tool: [register.py](../../tools/register.py).

## Establish scope and coverage

Identify the report/version, reporting entity, consolidated/standalone boundary, fiscal period, currency/scale, audit status and publication date. Distinguish the group from the borrower and guarantors. Retain PDF page numbers and printed labels when different.

Map every agreed part against actual pages: directors' and auditor's reports, statements, policies, notes and other included sections. A whole-report request covers the whole report; narrower scope must be explicit. Apply the workflow contract's source-hash and scope checks before reusing prior review.

Read every required part, preserving table continuations, headers, footnotes and cross-references. Record read, pending or unreadable coverage. Extraction alone is not substantive review.

When extraction loses signs, columns, scale or scanned text, inspect the relevant page images or available OCR and compare to the source. If recovery is unavailable, retain the gap. Never mark an unreadable passage not disclosed or infer a zero from an empty cell.

## Review the disclosures

Check these disclosures while completing the agreed reading:

- audit opinion, key audit matters, going concern and subsequent events;
- accounting policies/changes, estimates, restatements and consolidation changes;
- revenue, segments, costs, exceptional items, provisions and impairment;
- receivables, inventories, payables, cash restrictions and cash-flow presentation;
- borrowings, leases, maturities, interest/FX exposure, covenants, security, breaches and waivers;
- related parties, ownership, guarantees, commitments, contingencies, litigation and tax.

Record found with locator, not applicable with basis, not disclosed after relevant reading, or unresolved. Follow contradictions and note cross-references; retain positive context as well as risk.

Compare material statement/note amounts to the spread only after matching entity, period, definitions and units. Preserve reported versus adjusted values and restated versus originally published comparatives. Use deterministic arithmetic for tie-outs; record explained differences and unresolved residuals without inserting plugs.

## Deliver

Return coverage, located material findings with decisive passage/table details and qualifications, reconciliation issues and evidence gaps. Digests support navigation; they do not replace source evidence. Audited inclusion does not verify every management explanation or forecast.

Flag implications for financial-performance-analysis and repayment-and-structure. For changed reports, assess effects on existing conclusions, including previously unlinked adverse evidence.
