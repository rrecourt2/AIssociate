# Repayment and Structure

Explain how the borrower repays, what that conclusion depends on and whether the terms address the risks.

Apply [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and the [evidence workflow](../../shared/workflow-contract.md). Use the proposed terms, group structure, debt schedule, approved scenarios, liquidity evidence and agreements or legal input. Use institutional specialist methods where corporate debt-service analysis is unsuitable.

Tool: [register.py](../../tools/register.py).

## Assess repayment

Consult the relevant sections of [repayment-methods.md](../../shared/repayment-methods.md) for funding, debt service, contractual headroom and protections.

Map obligations and cash to the borrower, guarantors and relevant subsidiaries. Reconcile sources and uses and the proposed terms with existing debt, maturities and refinancing needs. Assess the timing of capex and working capital, tenor, amortisation, grace periods and currency or rate exposure where material.

Use approved projections and existing model outputs, keeping each scenario's provenance and assumptions. Propose assumption changes separately with their effects. Assess whether the base case is adequate and how the downside holds, identifying the trigger, timing and remaining funding gap. Net debt and historical EBITDA alone do not establish timely repayment.

Run requested illustrative sensitivities with explicit assumptions and deterministic arithmetic; label them illustrative until adopted. If essential forecasts are missing, state what remains unresolved without inventing a forecast or treating the gap as an automatic negative credit decision.

## Test terms and protections

Assess covenants under their contractual definitions; record actual compliance, forecast headroom and missing definitions separately.

Assess whether security, guarantees, covenants, reserves, information undertakings, staged disbursement and proposed conditions address the risk when needed. Use specialist evidence for legal conclusions.

Distinguish effective protections, conditions that must be met before reliance, and monitoring of continuing exposure. Keep the residual risk visible and identify conditions for the Investment Officer to adopt.

## Deliver

Return a located conclusion covering repayment dependencies, scenario assumptions, maturity and refinancing exposure, covenant results, effective protections and unresolved inputs, and record it in the register. Analysis, Investment Officer acceptance and Credit approval remain distinct.
