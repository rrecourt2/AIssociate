# Repayment and Structure

Explain how the borrower repays, what the conclusion depends on and whether the terms address the risks.

Apply [operating-rules.md](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and [workflow-contract.md](../../shared/workflow-contract.md). Use proposed terms, group structure, debt schedule, approved scenarios, liquidity evidence and agreements/legal input. Use institutional specialist methods where corporate debt-service analysis is unsuitable.

Tool: [register.py](../../tools/register.py).

## Assess repayment

Consult relevant sections of [repayment-methods.md](../../shared/repayment-methods.md) for funding, debt service, contractual headroom and protections.

Map obligations and cash to the borrower, guarantors and relevant subsidiaries. Reconcile sources/uses and proposed terms with existing debt, maturities and refinancing requirements. Assess capex/working-capital timing, tenor, amortisation, grace periods and currency/rate exposure where material.

Use approved projections and existing model outputs, retaining scenario provenance and assumptions. Propose assumption changes separately with their effects. Assess base-case adequacy and downside resilience, identifying the trigger, timing and remaining funding gap. Net debt and historical EBITDA alone do not establish timely repayment.

Use requested illustrative sensitivities with explicit assumptions and deterministic arithmetic; label them illustrative until adopted. If essential forecasts are missing, state what remains unresolved without inventing a forecast or treating the gap as an automatic negative credit decision.

## Test terms and protections

Assess covenants under their contractual definitions and record actual compliance, forecast headroom and missing definitions separately.

Assess whether security/guarantees, covenants, reserves, information undertakings, staged disbursement and proposed conditions address the risk when needed. Use specialist evidence for legal conclusions.

Distinguish effective protections, conditions that must be satisfied before reliance and monitoring of continuing exposure. Preserve residual risk and identify conditions for officer adoption.

## Deliver

Return a located conclusion covering repayment dependencies, scenario assumptions, maturity/refinancing exposure, covenant results, effective protections and unresolved inputs for the evidence owner to integrate. Reassess changed lender/legal/cash-flow evidence. Analysis, officer acceptance and Credit approval remain distinct.
