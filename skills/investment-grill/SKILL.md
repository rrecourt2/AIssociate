# Investment Grill

Test whether the conclusions are defensible for the requested decision and audience. A recommendation, where present, is a hypothesis to examine; neither approval, rejection nor finding a fault is the target.

Apply [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and the [review protocol](../../shared/review-protocol.md). Use the named version, brief, relevant analysis, original evidence and earlier findings. Record results through the [evidence workflow](../../shared/workflow-contract.md). Tools: [register.py](../../tools/register.py), [fp_docx.py](../../tools/fp_docx.py).

Review the case by default; use recheck for repairs and a fresh context for arbitration. In interview mode, ask one material question at a time. Otherwise batch essential gaps.

## Challenge what matters

Test the opportunity, alternatives and case at the agreed depth. Where relevant, examine business drivers, entity boundaries, cash generation, repayment/downside, proposed terms and effective protections. For Term Sheets/contracts, compare template deviations and proposed/agreed/executed terms; flag specialist questions rather than supplying legal clearance. Inspect originals for decisive and contrary evidence.

Watch for mismatched periods, units or definitions; assertions presented as proven causes; unsupported adjustments; unavailable cash/refinancing; and proposed conditions presented as existing protections. Check that compression or external presentation has not lost material limitations. A source tag or balanced calculation does not establish a sound conclusion.

Assess the whole paper as well as consequential issue batches. State whether an uncertainty needs evidence, an appropriate condition or Investment Officer judgment. Do not turn factual descriptions or wording preferences into risk findings.

## Return and recheck

Give each material finding an ID, version/location, concern, decision consequence, evidence/gap and resolution. Classify it as blocker, material improvement or later diligence for this decision, with a reason. Early-stage uncertainty is not automatically a blocker. Findings are proposals for the lead to assess and rewrite.

For Credit replies, check the actual question and whether the FP needs correction. Recheck repaired passages against evidence; mark resolved, open or Investment Officer judgment and retain the basis. Accept supported disagreement. Report inaccessible evidence and unperformed checks. If no material issue remains, say so.
