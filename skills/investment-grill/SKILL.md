# Investment Grill

Test whether the conclusions are defensible for the requested decision and audience. A recommendation, where present, is a hypothesis to examine; neither approval, rejection nor finding a fault is the target.

Apply [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and the [review protocol](../../shared/review-protocol.md). Use the named version, the brief, the relevant analysis, original evidence and earlier findings. Return findings to the main task, which records them through the [evidence workflow](../../shared/workflow-contract.md). Tool: [register.py](../../tools/register.py), to read the record.

Challenge the case by default; recheck repairs when asked, and use a fresh agent for arbitration. In interview mode, ask one material question at a time; otherwise batch the essential gaps.

## Challenge what matters

Test the opportunity, alternatives and case at the agreed depth. Where relevant, examine business drivers, entity boundaries, cash generation, repayment and downside, proposed terms and effective protections. For Term Sheets and contracts, compare template deviations and proposed, agreed and executed terms; flag specialist questions rather than supplying legal clearance. Inspect the originals for decisive and contrary evidence.

Watch for mismatched periods, units or definitions; assertions presented as proven causes; unsupported adjustments; unavailable cash or refinancing; and proposed conditions presented as existing protections. Check that compression or external presentation has not lost material limitations. A source tag or a balanced calculation does not make a conclusion sound.

Assess the whole paper and group related issues. State whether each uncertainty needs evidence, a condition or Investment Officer judgment. Do not turn factual descriptions or wording preferences into risk findings.

## Return and recheck

Give each material finding an ID, version and location, the concern, its consequence for the decision, the evidence or gap, and a proposed resolution. Classify it as a blocker, a material improvement or later diligence for this decision, with a reason. Early-stage uncertainty is not automatically a blocker. Findings are proposals for Document Lead to assess and rewrite.

For a Credit reply, check that it answers the actual question and whether the document needs correction. Recheck repaired passages against the evidence; mark each finding resolved, open or for Investment Officer judgment, with its basis. Accept supported disagreement. Report inaccessible evidence and checks not performed. If no material issue remains, say so.
