# Deal Updates and Guidance

Assess what correspondence changes in the investigation or financing case. Apply [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and the [evidence workflow](../../shared/workflow-contract.md), starting from the saved purpose, open items and coverage. Tool: [register.py](../../tools/register.py).

## Retrieve within scope

Check authorised received and sent mail since the last covered review, using the deal's aliases, parties and threads. Verify identity before merging similarly named deals. Without prior coverage, state the window you checked.

Read the relevant messages, their context and material attachments, not just snippets. Separate new text from quoted history. Keep the sender and role, message or thread ID, passage and attachment version; distinguish the sent date from the event date. Complete pagination within scope or disclose the gap; an empty search does not establish freshness.

Record the channels and coverage, and advance the cutoff only for completed scope. Without live mail, use the supplied exports and report the mailbox as unchecked. The plugin has no connector or scheduler of its own; do not promise monitoring.

## Attribute and interpret

Read a transcript fully before treating it as complete; record missing intervals, truncation and unclear speech. Recaps are secondary sources. For material points, keep the speaker and role, timestamp or paragraph, qualifications and version. Decide per passage whether the speaker is client, adviser or internal; do not guess who a room label represents.

Check consequential ambiguous figures, units, dates, negations or attributions against recordings or participants when available. Keep the original wording alongside corrections.

Distinguish assertions, hypotheses and questions, editorial suggestions, conditional advice, decisions and commitments. A question does not prove its premise, a suggestion is not adopted, and a promise is not received evidence. Keep fuzzy guidance and tentative interpretations; clarify when the interpretation would materially change the work. A decision needs an identified authorised decision-maker. Keep suggested owners and deadlines separate from agreed ones.

Prioritise by impact, urgency and dependencies. Recency, seniority, repetition and confidence do not establish truth.

## Reconcile and save

Check consequential claims against the original accounts, contracts and earlier statements, matching entity, period and definition. Record each as corroborated, attributed but unverified, conflicting or unresolved. Repeated or forwarded accounts are not independent corroboration.

Distinguish later developments from contradictions. A new message does not automatically supersede an executed agreement; keep the amendment's scope, history and unaffected obligations. Silence is not approval or waiver, and specialist discussion is not formal clearance.

Link the same issue across channels. A proposed change to the purpose or strategy belongs in the brief; only the Investment Officer adopts consequential changes.

Do not decide privilege yourself; use a cleared conclusion or keep the dependency in an authorised record.

Return the changes, original-source citations, checked and open claims, and the register updates with coverage. Separate internal actions, client questions and inputs for any requested document. Do not repeat a client request that current evidence already answers. Save through the evidence workflow; follow-up communications remain drafts.
