# Call with the CFO of Cedar Foods, 12 November 2026

Invented for the Bankability 2 example. Notes by the investment analyst.

- The CFO said the gross margin fell in 2025 because raw milk prices rose 18% during the drought.
- Cedar buys milk from three cooperatives. The contracts fix volumes, not prices.
- Bluegate pays within 45 days. Lakeside buys through a one-year contract. Three other distributors serve the same region.
- The CFO will send the supply contracts.
- About 6,000 smallholder farmers deliver through the three cooperatives. In the dry months, January to March, the plant gets about a third less milk.
- The second UHT line goes into the existing hall in Nakuru. It doubles capacity to 24,000 litres an hour. The first line has run close to full since 2024.
- The line costs EUR 14m. The loan pays EUR 12m and Cedar pays the rest from its own cash. The supplier is not chosen. Start-up is planned for the third quarter of 2027.
