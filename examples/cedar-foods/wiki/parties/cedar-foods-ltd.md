# Cedar Foods Ltd

## What it is

Cedar Foods Ltd is a dairy processor in Nakuru, Kenya, and the borrower of the proposed EUR 12m loan [F-001, F-006]. It has about 420 employees and sells in Kenya only [F-002, F-010].

## What we know

**How it earns its money.** Cedar buys raw milk from three cooperatives, makes UHT milk and yoghurt from it at one plant in Nakuru, and sells both to supermarkets and distributors in Kenya [F-019]. Revenue was KES 4,060m in 2025 and KES 3,720m in 2024 [F-008].

**Whom it depends on.** Two customers stand out. Bluegate Supermarkets bought 31% of revenue in 2025 and Lakeside Distributors 14% [F-009]. The CFO said Bluegate pays within 45 days [F-014]. Lakeside buys under a one-year contract and three other distributors serve the same region, so it has no page of its own [F-015, D-001]. On the supply side, about 6,000 smallholder farmers deliver through the three cooperatives [F-016].

**What is particular.**

- *The milk comes with the rain.* In the dry months, January to March, the plant gets about a third less milk [F-016]. The [second UHT line](../deal/second-uht-line.md) doubles what the plant can process, not what the farmers deliver.
- *Volumes are fixed and the price is open.* The contracts with the cooperatives fix how much Cedar takes and leave the price open [F-013], so a dry year reaches its costs at once. The CFO put the fall of the gross margin in 2025 down to an 18% rise in milk prices during the drought [F-012]. That is the CFO's account, given on one call. No document that shows the margin has been read.
- *It earns shillings and asks for euros.* All revenue is in Kenyan shillings [F-010], and so is all existing debt [F-011]. The loan is asked in euros [F-007].

**Debt.** Borrowings were KES 1,300m at 31 December 2025, all from Kenyan banks and in shillings [F-011].

## Assumptions and open points

- **The margin fell in 2025 because of milk prices, and not for a lasting reason.** This rests on what the CFO said on one call [F-012]. The management accounts for 2025 and 2026 would test it.
- **A loan in euros can be serviced from revenue in shillings.** Nothing supports this yet [F-007, F-010]. Cedar is to propose how it will cover the loan before the concept note goes in [D-002].
- **Unknown:** whether the supply contracts cap the milk price. The contracts are asked for [Q-001].
- **Unknown:** whether Cedar can pass a higher milk price on to Bluegate and its other customers.
- **Unknown:** who the Kenyan lenders are and what security they hold [F-011].
- **Unknown:** which company guarantees the loan [F-006].
