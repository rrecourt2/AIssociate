# Second UHT line

## What it is

The second UHT line is the project that the proposed loan pays for: a second processing line in Cedar's existing hall in Nakuru [F-017, F-006]. It is planned and not yet ordered [F-018].

## What we know

**The plan.** The line goes into the hall that houses the first one and doubles capacity to 24,000 litres an hour [F-017]. It costs EUR 14m. The loan pays EUR 12m and [Cedar Foods Ltd](../parties/cedar-foods-ltd.md) pays the rest from its own cash [F-018]. Start-up is planned for the third quarter of 2027. The supplier is not chosen [F-018].

**Why now.** The CFO said the first line has run close to full since 2024 [F-017].

**What is particular.** The line adds capacity to process milk, and the milk has to follow. Cedar gets about a third less milk in the dry months, January to March [F-016]. On those figures a plant with twice the capacity stands partly idle in those months unless more milk is found. Nothing read so far says where the extra milk comes from, or who buys the extra product.

## Assumptions and open points

- **The extra capacity can be filled and sold.** Nothing supports this yet. It needs a view on milk supply beyond the three cooperatives and on demand beyond the present customers.
- **EUR 14m is enough.** The figure is the CFO's, given on one call, before a supplier is chosen [F-018].
- **Unknown:** what Cedar pays its own share from, beside borrowings of KES 1,300m [F-011].
- **Unknown:** the permits, the works in the hall, and who installs the line.
