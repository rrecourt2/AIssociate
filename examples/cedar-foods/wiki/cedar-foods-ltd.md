# Cedar Foods Ltd

## What it is

Cedar Foods Ltd is a dairy processor in Nakuru, Kenya, and the borrower of the proposed EUR 12m loan [F-001, F-006]. It sells UHT milk and yoghurt in Kenya only [F-010].

## What we know

**Revenue and customers.** Revenue was KES 4,060m in 2025 and KES 3,720m in 2024 [F-008]. Bluegate Supermarkets bought 31% of it and Lakeside Distributors 14% [F-009]. All revenue is in Kenyan shillings [F-010].

**Costs.** Cedar buys raw milk from three cooperatives. The contracts fix volumes and leave the price open [F-013]. The CFO said the gross margin fell in 2025 because raw milk prices rose 18% during the drought [F-012]. This is the CFO's account, given on one call. No document that shows the margin has been read.

**Debt.** Borrowings were KES 1,300m at 31 December 2025, all from Kenyan banks and in shillings [F-011].

## Assumptions and open points

- **The margin fell in 2025 because of milk prices, and not for a lasting reason.** This rests on what the CFO said on one call [F-012]. The management accounts for 2025 and 2026 would test it.
- **A loan in euros can be serviced from revenue in shillings.** Nothing supports this yet. Revenue is in shillings only [F-010] and the loan is asked in euros [F-007].
- **Unknown:** whether the supply contracts cap the milk price. The contracts are asked for [Q-001].
- **Unknown:** which company guarantees the loan.
