# Cedar Foods

## What it is

Cedar Foods Ltd, a dairy processor in Nakuru, Kenya, asks for a senior loan of EUR 12m over 7 years to add a second UHT line [F-001, F-006].

## What we know

**The request.** [Cedar Foods Ltd](../parties/cedar-foods-ltd.md) borrows. The money pays for the [second UHT line](second-uht-line.md), which costs EUR 14m: EUR 12m from the loan and the rest from Cedar's own cash [F-018]. A group company is asked to guarantee the loan. Which one is not decided [F-006].

**How the loan is to be repaid.** From the cash that Cedar earns on its sales of milk and yoghurt in Kenya [F-019]. Revenue was KES 4,060m in 2025 [F-008]. The second way out is the guarantee, and no company stands behind it yet [F-006].

**What stands in the way so far.** Cedar earns shillings and would owe euros [F-010, F-007]. Its margin fell in 2025, which the CFO put down to the price of raw milk in the drought [F-012]. Both are described on the page of [Cedar Foods Ltd](../parties/cedar-foods-ltd.md).

## Assumptions and open points

- **Cedar's cash flow carries the loan.** Not tested. Only revenue and borrowings are known [F-008, F-011]. No profit, cash flow or debt service has been read.
- **The guarantee comes from a company that can pay.** Nothing supports this yet. The guarantor is not named [F-006].
- **Agreed:** Cedar proposes how it will cover the euro loan before the concept note goes in [D-002].
