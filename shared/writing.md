# Writing the financing paper

Approved examples set the voice, structure within sections, terminology and level of detail. Use one primary example, with others for specific sections. Examples supply style, never another deal's facts. Without examples, report that house style is uncalibrated.

Use **ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English)** selectively when explaining unfamiliar concepts or mechanisms. The examples always take priority. Do not apply it across the paper or impose a word list, sentence limit or compliance target. Preserve natural connections, familiar institutional wording and necessary qualifications.

Write the strongest defensible case for the officer's provisional recommendation. Keep facts that could change the decision visible. Explain observations, material risks, relevant mitigants and residual exposure where needed; this is reasoning, not compulsory headings or a paragraph formula. Describe the company, supply chain and balance sheet in their natural order without manufacturing risks.

Explain material financial movements, supported drivers and their implications for cash and repayment. Distinguish existing protections from proposed conditions and assumptions. Do not force an acceptable conclusion where the evidence leaves a material question open.

The FP contains the financing argument and necessary qualifications. Analysis holds fuller reasoning, reconciliations and internal tasks; the client DDQ contains targeted evidence requests. Select and rewrite specialist inputs instead of pasting their workplans into the paper. Each annex answers its agreed question; summarise decision-relevant results in the body.

Review whole sections for flow, density, repetition and fit beside the examples. Preserve attribution, uncertainty, scope and meaning during edits. Unsupported claims return for substantive review; smoother wording is not stronger evidence.
