# Review protocol

Use fresh challenge for the case/section plan, consequential analysis batches and the complete FP with annexes. Group related issues; descriptive paragraphs do not each need an agent. Recheck material revisions and their consequences elsewhere, reusing unaffected checks.

Give the reviewer the exact version, brief, relevant original evidence/calculations, contrary evidence, open questions and enough wider context to find omissions. Keep it separate from the drafting conversation. The reviewer may agree with a sound conclusion.

Default to one challenge round and one focused recheck. The author repairs findings or gives an evidence-backed response; useful drafting elsewhere continues. FP Lead selects and rewrites adopted findings. Questions do not automatically become conditions or repeated caveats.

Use a third fresh arbiter for unresolved material disagreement or an officer-requested adjudication. It reads the decisive evidence before comparing positions and returns a scoped conclusion, correction or missing input. Do not manufacture consensus. The officer retains recommendation and risk-acceptance decisions.

Stop when the evidence gap or officer decision is clear; further rounds need new evidence or a material unresolved point. Record findings and dispositions in the existing register. If separate tasks are unavailable, give a named handoff and label self-review honestly. Agents are not independent professional assurance.
