# Repayment and structure methods

Apply actual agreements and institution-approved model conventions.

## Sources, uses and timing

Separate financing at close from ongoing debt service. Cover uses when due, including fees, refinancing and contingencies. Equity pledged in a term sheet may not be paid-in or available; revolver-funded repayment remains debt and may only move a maturity.

At the paying obligor, check bank-account ownership, restrictions, consents, permitted transfers and evidence dates. Do not double count subsidiary cash as group liquidity and upstream dividends, or duplicate cash, operating inflows and funding proceeds. A guarantee may support recovery without timely payment liquidity.

Inspect intra-year cash troughs where seasonality or maturities matter; positive year-end cash can hide earlier shortfalls. Match committed facilities' availability, expiry, draw conditions and covenants to the need. Keep indicative/conditional refinancing separate from available funding.

## Debt service

Define cash available for debt service and the matching entity/period. DSCR needs its defined numerator and scheduled principal/interest denominator; EBITDA/interest is not DSCR. Include balloons when due and leases/fixed charges under the adopted method, avoiding duplicate interest or principal adjustments.

Separate operating cash, asset disposal, sponsor support and refinancing. Identify each non-operating source's commitment, execution risk, timing and importance to the recommendation. Refinancing dependence does not demonstrate amortisation from operations.

Downside should test the mechanism and interactions: weaker margins can change working-capital needs; capex delays can defer revenue and increase interest. Consider collections, commissioning, rates/FX and refinancing delays where material.

## Covenant headroom

Retain agreement/clause, obligor/perimeter, numerator/denominator, test date/frequency, limits, adjustments, cure provisions and waiver evidence. Maximum-ratio headroom is ceiling minus ratio; minimum-ratio headroom is ratio minus floor. Negative headroom is not available capacity.

A ratio gap becomes borrowing capacity only under explicit numerator/denominator assumptions and other constraints. Zero/negative denominators may make a mechanical ratio meaningless. Escalate ambiguous definitions rather than substituting a convenient group ratio. Apply waivers only to their stated breach, entity and period.

## Protection and conditions

For security/guarantees, obtain specialist evidence on obligor, ranking, perfection, value/recoverability, caps, expiry and jurisdiction/enforcement constraints. Separate effective protection, arrangements signed but not yet effective, and proposals. Monitoring covenants detect risk and provide remedies; they do not eliminate commercial exposure.

State a condition's risk, required action/evidence, responsible party and deadline/event, including whether it precedes approval, signing or disbursement. A promise to discuss refinancing is not equivalent to committed funding before maturity. Do not claim a facility committed, waiver granted or condition met without evidence.
