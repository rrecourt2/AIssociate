# One real-deal pilot

Use a real partially completed FP, its separate template, original evidence, checked financial export and approved examples. Keep inputs and results in the approved environment. Success means a useful complete workflow. Stage updates should identify actual inputs and verified outputs without routine approval pauses.

1. **Confirm access and installation.** Record `fp.py --version`, script hashes and model. Confirm permitted folders, connectors, session storage and retention. Probe the actual Word template and identify unsupported edits before drafting.
2. **Build and save the evidence index.** Use relevant specialists, with one register writer. Retain the purpose, hypotheses and decisions. Integrate material findings from each workstream or state what remains outside the record. Save the register and readable Analysis in the authorised location; include other exports when useful.
3. **Resume before writing.** In a fresh task, retrieve the record, recover the purpose and next decision, follow a fact to its original passage/cell and identify what remains missing. Reuse unchanged material. This must work without rereading the whole deal folder.
4. **Draft and review the whole FP.** Use the saved record, template and examples. Assess the business account, financial drivers, cash/repayment logic, contrary evidence and natural house style. Check for internal-task leakage, repeated caveats and unsupported conditions. Verify Word layout and retrieve the complete saved bundle.
5. **Make a normal update.** Edit a section in Word and add a comment. Add a real new email, document or answer. Continue in a fresh task: revise affected work, preserve the officer edits and assess the answer without repeating closed client requests. Exercise Save As if normally used.

Record exact failures, officer correction time, elapsed time and actual available credit usage. Compare the result with the officer's ordinary workflow. Fix observed failures before expanding scope; synthetic checks do not establish writing quality or tenant operation.
