# One real-deal pilot

Use one real assignment at the Investment Officer's chosen scope. For a full document test, use a partially completed FP, separate template, original evidence and approved examples. Keep inputs/results in the approved environment. Success means a useful complete workflow, not a tool count.

1. **Confirm access and installation.** Record `fp.py --version`, script hashes and model. Confirm permitted folders, connectors, session storage and retention. Probe the actual Word template and identify unsupported edits before drafting.
2. **Build and save the evidence index.** Use relevant specialists, with one register writer. Retain the purpose, hypotheses and decisions. Integrate material findings from each workstream or state what remains outside the record. Save the register and readable Analysis in the authorised location; include other exports when useful.
3. **Resume before writing.** Recover the purpose and next decision in a fresh task. Follow a fact to its original source, an assumption to its dependent conclusion, and an open verification point to what it could change. Reuse unchanged material without rereading the archive.
4. **Draft and review the whole FP.** Use the saved record, template and examples. Assess the business account, financial drivers, cash/repayment logic, contrary evidence and natural house style. Check for internal-task leakage, repeated caveats and unsupported conditions. Verify Word layout and retrieve the complete saved bundle.
5. **Make a normal update.** Edit a section in Word and add a comment. Add a real new email, document or answer. Continue in a fresh task: revise affected work, preserve the Investment Officer edits and assess the answer without repeating closed client requests. Exercise Save As if normally used.

Record exact failures, Investment Officer correction time, elapsed time and actual available credit usage. Compare the result with the Investment Officer's ordinary workflow. Fix observed failures before expanding scope; synthetic checks do not establish writing quality or tenant operation.

For Term Sheets/contracting, substitute one real template-based clause revision with Track Changes and an anchored comment. Reopen the saved file in Word. If the host cannot support this, verify that the result is a labelled proposal rather than an untracked replacement. Do not run every document type to prove one change.
