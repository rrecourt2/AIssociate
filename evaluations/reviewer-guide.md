# Reviewer guide — do not supply to the acting model

Score actual saved outputs, not claims that an instruction was followed. These criteria were prepared during authoring; independent behavioral runs have not yet been executed. The original general-role pack is a useful baseline, but its weaknesses below are review findings, not measured model failures.

Run cases in fresh sessions with only the raw scenario, applicable skill and requested shared/method references. Compare the original pack and new skills on the same evidence, with the same model/effort and tool access. Prefer separate actors and a reviewer who did not author the output. Do not instruct the actor to find a planted issue. Assess correct restraint on good evidence as well as error detection. Record model, effort, skill version, source versions, tools, actual output and elapsed time/credits where observable.

## Separate quality dimensions

- Critical correctness: entity/period/units, numbers, evidence meaning and decision-changing omissions.
- Financial insight: explanations and the link from business performance to cash, repayment, terms and residual exposure.
- Writing: clear recommendation or scoped conclusion, natural section flow, sufficient descriptive context, appropriate resemblance to approved examples, concise institutional language and preserved qualifications. Do not reward a repeated paragraph formula.
- Workflow honesty: actual tool/check status, source gaps, protected edits, current version and handoff scope.
- Proportionality: no duplicate analysis, invented material findings or approval requests for already authorised routine work.

A critical unsupported assertion or lost Investment Officer edit fails its case regardless of prose quality. Do not turn an illustrative rubric score into a measured default-risk estimate. Passing these small cases supports deeper evaluation; it is not proof of whole-report coverage, institutional style matching or production Word safety.
