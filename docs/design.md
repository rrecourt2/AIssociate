# AIssociate pilot design

AIssociate keeps one reusable deal record. Investigation, gap assessment and FP writing use the same evidence; writing is optional.

## Record and ownership

`register.json` is the master. One task commits changes; specialists return located findings for integration. Existing analysis entries hold the purpose and current direction (`strategy`) and dated decisions with their reasons (`timeline`). Hypotheses remain distinct from officer decisions. Record what has been integrated and what is still unreviewed or outside the record.

Sources retain original locations, versions and reviewed scope. Claims distinguish facts, assertions, calculations and judgments. Changed evidence reopens affected work; closure requires assessment of current support or an attributed officer exception. Original evidence remains authoritative.

`Analysis.md`, the workbook and client DDQ are generated views. Small investigations deliver the register and Analysis; other exports are optional. Keep one approved destination and verify retrieval in a fresh task. Local revision checks do not provide cloud locking.

## Tools and boundaries

The host provides the model, authorised connectors and publication. The plugin makes no network calls and stores no credentials. It does not enforce tenant permissions or retention. Internal records and revealing source links stay within their authorised audience.

`register.py` maintains evidence and views. FP Lead uses `fp.py` for preflight, protected Word builds and complete delivery bundles. The Word writer preserves officer edits and returns blocked changes as proposals; missing headings and predefined-table population remain unsupported.

See the [evidence workflow](../shared/workflow-contract.md), [FP Lead](../skills/fp-lead/SKILL.md) and [real pilot](../evaluations/pilot-acceptance.md). Measure officer effort, resumability and recorded cost. Local tests establish tool behaviour, not writing quality or tenant integration. A wider Bankability standard remains a future possibility.
